5. IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
------------------------
The dataset documentation states that only transactions flagged
by the source system were manually reviewed. Other transactions
were automatically labelled legitimate without individual review.

This creates verification bias. Some actual fraud may therefore
be incorrectly labelled as legitimate.

The results measure agreement with the dataset's recorded labels.
They do not prove real-world banking performance.

The external dataset is a public demonstration dataset, not
verified transaction data from a licensed bank.

6. CONCLUSION
-------------
The saved Random Forest model achieved high scores against the
external dataset's recorded labels.

Further evaluation using independently verified, representative
banking data is necessary before making claims about real-world
fraud detection performance.
